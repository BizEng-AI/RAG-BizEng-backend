Sample business negotiation playbook

Opening a negotiation meeting:
- Thank you for joining today. I would like to confirm our agenda before we begin.
- Our goal is to review pricing, delivery timing, and support responsibilities.
- If that works for you, I can start with the proposal summary.

Making a counteroffer politely:
- I understand your position. From our perspective, the current price is above budget.
- Would you consider a revised unit price if we increase the order volume?
- We are willing to proceed provided that the delivery timeline is realistic.

Clarifying delivery trade-offs:
- Air freight is faster, but rail is often more cost-effective for planned shipments.
- Rail can offer better value when the cargo is not urgent and the route is stable.
- To reduce risk, we should confirm customs documents and add a schedule buffer.

Closing the discussion:
- Let me summarize the main points we agreed on today.
- We have a provisional agreement on price, timeline, and next actions.
- I will send a follow-up email with the updated terms and owners by Friday.
