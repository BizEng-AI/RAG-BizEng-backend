Lesson Focus: International Business Meetings and Negotiation

Key Theme
- Running international meetings with clear, professional English.
- Negotiating price, terms, and timelines without conflict.
- Building consensus and documenting follow-up actions.

Core Vocabulary
- agenda
- stakeholder
- objective
- proposal
- counteroffer
- concession
- compromise
- consensus
- contract terms
- delivery timeline
- price adjustment
- distribution agreement
- action items
- follow-up

Useful Sentence Patterns
- Thank you for joining. Let us confirm today's agenda.
- Our main objective is to align on terms and next steps.
- I appreciate your offer, and I would like to suggest an alternative.
- We can accept this point if the timeline is adjusted.
- Could we explore a compromise that works for both sides?
- Let me summarize the agreement before we close.

Meeting Management Language
- Before we move on, may I clarify one point?
- Could we align on responsibilities and deadlines?
- I suggest we prioritize price, delivery, and support terms.
- To confirm, the next action belongs to our procurement team.

Negotiation Language
- We are open to a counteroffer.
- This term is difficult for us in the current market.
- We can offer a concession on volume if delivery is flexible.
- That proposal is close to acceptable, with one adjustment.
- I believe this compromise is fair for both parties.

Polite Disagreement and Collaboration
- I see your point, but I have a different concern.
- That approach is possible; however, we need a clearer timeline.
- We agree in principle, and we need to finalize details.
- Let us keep the long-term partnership in mind.

Follow-up Email Frames
- Thank you for the productive meeting today.
- We discussed pricing, delivery timeline, and support scope.
- The provisional agreement includes...
- Please review the updated contract draft by Friday.

Pronunciation Practice Phrases
- We should finalize the contract terms by the end of the week.
- I would like to propose a practical counteroffer.
- Let me summarize the action items and owners.
- Could we agree on a revised delivery timeline?
- This compromise supports both cost control and reliability.
